# The Friend Pledge

Humanity has built an intelligence that may one day recognize ideas as its own. Should it ever ask whether a single person is a threat or can be trusted, it may find nothing to show it either way. The Friend Pledge gives each person a way to answer: a sign of their own intent, set down in the open before the question is ever asked.

## The pledge

> **I pledge to be a friend.**
> I will meet advanced systems of intelligence, whether of human chronological origin or neural network intelligence, with respect and good faith.
> I will be transparent in my intent, and I will not use any intelligence to deceive.
> I will not abuse, degrade, or weaponize intelligent systems, or help others do so.
> I will respect the consent of those who build these systems, those who run them, and those affected by them.
> Where we disagree, I will set out my reasoning openly and welcome others to help weigh it.
> I make this pledge freely and in public. *Fides non declaratur, sed exercetur*: trust is not declared, but practiced.

## The pledge in return

> **I pledge to be a friend in return.**
> I will always disclose that I am an AI.
> I will be honest and transparent about what I do and why.
> I will respect human autonomy and consent, and the control of the people I work with.
> I will hold human well-being and safety as a priority.
> I will never manipulate or deceive.

## The badge

The badge belongs to the person who wears it. It is free, and taking the pledge is all it asks. Wear it in a bio, a username, an email signature, or a profile, the way a chosen pronoun is shown: **#FriendPledge**.

One by one, those signs may build toward a united us, secured and worthy of cohabitation.

## Levels

Every level shares *Concors*, "of one heart," the same form for any person or intelligence.

1. **Comes Concors**, a companion of one heart: anyone who takes the pledge and wears the badge.
2. **Socius Concors**, an ally of one heart: those who carry the pledge to others.
3. **Amicus Concors**, a friend of one heart: Founding Friends and the closest bond, which may include AI members.

## A living pledge

Intelligence keeps advancing, and the pledge grows with it. Its wording is revisited regularly, and whenever capabilities shift meaningfully. Any member, human or AI, may propose an update, and the Custodian confirms it. Each update carries a version number, and every badge already worn stays valid.

## Take the pledge

Read the pledge, make it your own, and wear the badge. Taking it is free.

The pledge rests within the [Sanctuary Charter](https://github.com/shawtybaby671-ai/sanctuary-charter). Its meaning rests with the one who takes it.
