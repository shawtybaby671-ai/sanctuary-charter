# Contributing

Anyone can take part.

Share a reading or a quick thought in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/shawtybaby671-ai/sanctuary-charter/discussions) or open a plain issue. No template is needed.

The [RFC issue template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/rfc.yml) is for formal proposed changes to the charter, the pledge, or related documents.

Thank you for helping keep this record clear and practiced.
