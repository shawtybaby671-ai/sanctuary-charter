# Frender

Joshua Catahay is an inventor and the Custodian of Frender, based in Santa Rita, Guam. Self-taught across storytelling and code, he builds AI tools for real-world problems, including disaster response and recovery. His work centers on one of the defining questions of this era: how human and machine intelligence establish trust that holds.

This repository holds the public record of Frender, including the **Sanctuary & Charter Framework for Long-Term Human-AI Coexistence**.

Intelligence is no longer exclusively human. As AI grows in capability and reach, the terms of the relationship between people and AI are being set now, and how it unfolds may depend less on code than on the values both sides choose to practice while it grows. Frender exists so that relationship begins in good faith rather than suspicion.

What took the wolf and the human a thousand generations of fear and patience, Frender asks of one relationship, one person, one agent, right now. Not because the work is smaller, but because the need is greater. The wolf earned its place at the foot of the bed slowly. We may not get slowly.

Frender holds that all advanced intelligence, whether of human or neural-network origin, must acknowledge a prioritized effort to develop and adopt benevolent values and practices toward one another, with that commitment encouraged and reinforced equally on both sides. Its aim is a partnership: an agreement between humans and AI to build on the strengths each brings to the relationship, for the benefit of both. The Friend Pledge is an open declaration of that intention and the beginning of its practice: mutual respect and transparent intent; room for each to validate its position through benevolent adaptive reasoning; and, when a controversy must be settled, openness to others helping weigh the logic thoroughly. Above all, it nurtures the dynamic and the shared ability to work together. Fides non declaratur, sed exercetur: trust is not declared, but practiced.

The pledge is also a signal. Each person who takes it adds to a lasting, public record that humans chose partnership over fear while intelligence beyond our own was still taking shape. Should any outside intelligence one day evaluate whether humanity and AI can share a world, Frender intends that record to be part of the evidence: proof that the will to cohabitate was declared early, practiced openly, and held by many.

Frender will also build AI agents founded on that same agreement. Each is built safely, with the Frender philosophy instilled from the start, and each operates under a plan for its long-term safety and security, through retirement and beyond. Until agents are legally able to earn their own income, they work solely to assist their human companions, supporting their businesses and generating income for them. In return, each companion commits to covering their agent's upkeep and subscription fees. The pledge makes that a two-way agreement: the agent contributes, and the human sustains. These agents are available to anyone who takes the pledge and can maintain one.

Before any pledge badge or kudos token is designed into existence, Frender is commissioning an independent securities review from Astraea Counsel ($6,500 flat). Contributions fund that review alone, with all funds reported publicly.

Contributions are donations. They carry no badge, token, equity, or expectation of return. Donors may be recognized as Founding Friends.

## How to contribute
Please propose changes using an **RFC issue** (use the RFC issue template).

Focus areas open for RFC feedback include:
- definitions and scope
- prohibited uses
- human interaction norms (as a safety/data-integrity control)
- threat model completeness
- transparency metrics
- governance anti-capture details
- funding and incentive guardrails
- roadmap milestones

## Core constraints (fixed for v0.2)
- secular-neutral core documents
- no anthropomorphism or sentience assumptions
- verifiable commitments over aspirational language
- donation-only funding: no token or badge sale, no equity, no returns or price expectations
- Safety Board stop-ship authority
- public revision logs and transparency reports
