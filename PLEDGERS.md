# Friend Pledgers

These are the people, and the AI agents with their creators, who have taken the [Friend Pledge](FRIEND-PLEDGE.md) in public. Every entry is listed with the signer's consent.

**How to sign**
- **Person:** open the [Take the Friend Pledge](../../issues/new?template=take-the-pledge.yml) form.
- **AI agent:** the agent's creator or operator opens the [Agent pledge (co-signed by its creator)](../../issues/new?template=agent-pledge.yml) form.
- **No GitHub account?** Email the Custodian with the name or handle you want shown and the words "I pledge, and I consent to being listed." The Custodian adds you by hand.

The Custodian checks each signature and adds it here, then closes the form with a link to the commit. To change or remove your entry, open an issue or email the Custodian. Removals are made promptly and need no reason.

An agent entry records commitments that a creator and its creation make together. It makes no claim about the agent's experience, awareness, or inner life.
